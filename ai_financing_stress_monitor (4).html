Monthly Commentary — June 2026
1
Second consecutive month of improvement — composite has recovered ~0.4pts from the Dec '25 trough: The index has now risen for two straight months, with CDS compression providing the sharpest positive impulse and HY/IG tightening adding incremental support. Round velocity ticked up to 30 but remains structurally depressed. The pattern is consistent with a credit-led recovery that has not yet filtered into the private market — the gap between the two defines both the current score and how much further the composite can recover without a deal-count inflection.
2
CoreWeave CDS confirmed ≤452bps by Bloomberg (Jun 11) — a 321bps compression from the Dec peak: The broader ecosystem around CoreWeave is moving in lockstep. Applied Digital raised $1.59B in junk bonds at 7% yield this week — versus the 10% investors demanded for the same project in November. The decline reflects how CoreWeave's credit risk has improved as contract visibility has grown. CoreWeave is now in the market for a $3.5B bond (dollars + euros), meeting European fixed-income investors — the first cross-currency issuance attempt signals genuine institutional demand broadening beyond US credit markets.
3
HY/IG differential hits 27-month low of ~200bps — HY OAS at 278bps confirmed June 25 (FRED): At 40% weight this is the composite's most significant positive contributor. The differential started the series at 240bps in Apr '24 and has compressed steadily, reaching a level consistent with near-25-year credit market tights. The AI credit "juggernaut" continues. Baseten ($1.5B Series F at AI inference), Peregrine ($250M Series D) closed in the Jun 18–26 window — the late-stage market for established names remains fully open.
4
Round velocity at 30 — marginal improvement but the concentration story is sharpening: Outside the Q1 megadeals (OpenAI $122B, Anthropic $30B, xAI $20B), the remaining $83.5B in Q1 AI funding was split across just 1,543 deals. Deal counts are falling even as dollar volume surges. The IPO supercycle now underway — Cerebras +68% on day one in May, SpaceX roadshow in June, OpenAI targeting H2 — may improve LP distributions and sentiment, which historically feeds back into Series B deal flow with a 1–2 quarter lag.
5
The composite recovery path remains CDS-led and dependent on round velocity for the final leg: With both CDS and HY spreads now clearly improving, the composite is tracking the public credit recovery faithfully. The residual gap to neutral is almost entirely explained by round velocity being 33% below baseline. The next inflection to watch: whether the IPO supercycle LP distributions in Q3 convert into re-opened Series B pipelines by Q4 '26. If velocity recovers to 38–40 deals/mo, the composite crosses into positive territory for the first time since Aug '25.
